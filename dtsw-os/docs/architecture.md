# DTSW Architecture

DTSW has 4 major pillars.

1. CLUBS (CLT)
- AI & Innovation Club
- Startup & Entrepreneurship Club
- Ambassador Community
- Media & Content Team

2. RESEARCH CELL (SCD)
- Student research projects
- Idea exploration
- Problem statements
- Research collaboration

3. INDUSTRY LAB (IIPC)
- Industry problem solving
- Real-world projects
- Internship opportunities
- Industry collaboration

4. DTSW TALKS
- Future TEDx-style talks
- Founder sessions
- Podcast-style discussions
- Community conversations

System Roles:
- Student Member
- Core Team
- Mentor/Admin

Platform Flow:
Student → Apply → AI Screening → Mentor Review → Club Access → Tasks/Events → Performance Tracking → Leadership Opportunities
