# DTSW Vision

DT Student Wing (DTSW) is a student-driven innovation ecosystem under the SNS Institutions culture and Design Thinking framework.

Tagline:
Learn. Build. Contribute.

Mission:
To empower students beyond academics by connecting learning, innovation, industry exposure, startups, leadership, and real-world execution into one unified platform.

Core Objective:
- Build innovation culture among students
- Activate student clubs with execution-focused activities
- Connect students with industry opportunities
- Build a scalable student innovation ecosystem
- Support SNS vision of “1000 AI Startups in 10 Years”

DTSW acts as:
- Student execution engine
- Innovation community
- Collaboration platform
- Leadership ecosystem
- Future startup pipeline

Inspired by:
- SNS Design Thinking ecosystem
- SNS iHub culture
- Industry-driven innovation systems
- IIT/IIM innovation communities
- GDG communities
- Research-driven student ecosystems
